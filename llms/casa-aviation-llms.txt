# Civil Aviation Safety Authority (CASA)

> Australia's independent statutory aviation safety regulator, established under the Civil Aviation Act 1988. CASA maintains the Australian Civil Aircraft Register (VH- marks), issues Airworthiness Directives, licenses flight crew and maintenance organisations, certifies air operators, and regulates drone and RPAS operations. CASA publishes no developer portal and no OpenAPI. Its machine-consumable surface is two open, no-key bulk data products served from services.casa.gov.au, plus one approval-gated partner API (the CASA RPAS Digital Platform).

## APIs

- [CASA Airworthiness Directives Data Files](https://www.casa.gov.au/aircraft/airworthiness/airworthiness-directives/data-files-all-airworthiness-directives): Open, unauthenticated bulk files covering every current Australian Airworthiness Directive. Base host https://services.casa.gov.au. No API key, no registration, no terms acceptance.
- [Australian Civil Aircraft Register Data Files](https://www.casa.gov.au/aircraft/aircraft-registration/data-files-registered-aircraft): The complete Australian civil aircraft register as a daily comma-delimited file (~7.6MB) and a zip equivalent (~1MB). Published under CASR 47.030. Base host https://services.casa.gov.au/CSV.
- [CASA RPAS Digital Platform API](https://www.casa.gov.au/drones/industry-initiatives/digital-platform): Partner-only API supplying CASA advisories, notifications and geospatial map data to drone safety applications, and processing Automated Airspace Authorisations. Base host https://data.casa.rpasplatform.net. Requires a service account, a signed four-year agreement, a CASA onboarding check-out and a paid Airservices Australia airspace data licence.

## Data files

- [combinedadweb.json](https://services.casa.gov.au/JSON/combinedadweb.json): All current Airworthiness Directives with aircraft and equipment references. Bespoke `{"DATA":[...]}` envelope. CASA states the format is subject to change.
- [adweb.csv](https://services.casa.gov.au/airworth/airwd/data/adweb.csv): All current Airworthiness Directives, CSV.
- [folder.csv](https://services.casa.gov.au/airworth/airwd/data/folder.csv): Aircraft and equipment types, CSV.
- [acrftreg.csv](https://services.casa.gov.au/CSV/acrftreg.csv): Full Australian civil aircraft register, updated daily.
- [acrftreg.zip](https://services.casa.gov.au/CSV/acrftreg.zip): Zip-compressed equivalent of acrftreg.csv.

Individual Airworthiness Directive documents are addressable as PDFs under
`https://services.casa.gov.au/airworth/airwd/ADfiles/<folder>/<file>.pdf`, keyed by
the `FOLDERNAME` and `ADFILE` fields in combinedadweb.json.

## Notes for agents

- There is no OpenAPI, Swagger, AsyncAPI, GraphQL schema, MCP server, JSON Schema or Postman collection published by CASA. Re-verified exhaustively on 2026-07-28 across all four CASA hosts (36 probes, zero hits), and CASA mirrors nothing to data.gov.au.
- POLL CONDITIONALLY. Both open files serve `ETag` and `Last-Modified`, and both `If-None-Match` and `If-Modified-Since` return a real `304` with an empty body (verified 2026-07-28). The host offers no gzip, so an unconditional daily poll costs 14MB for nothing. `HEAD` and `Range` also work.
- Filter airworthiness directives on `Status in ("Active", "Urgent")`. `Cancelled` is 24.4% of the file, and `Urgent` is a separate status, not a flag on `Active` - filtering for `Active` alone drops all 419 urgent directives.
- The aircraft register CSV has a UTF-8 BOM (read it as `utf-8-sig`) and Australian `DD/MM/YYYY` dates. The AD product uses ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`. The two disagree.
- The register carries the name and postal address of every registration holder, and roughly a third are private individuals. Treat it as personal information; do not republish rows wholesale.
- Nothing joins the airworthiness data to the aircraft register. "Which directives apply to VH-XYZ" is not answerable from the published data.
- The two open products are whole-file downloads, not query APIs: there is no pagination, no filtering and no per-record REST endpoint. Fetch the file, then filter locally.
- `https://www.casa.gov.au/search-centre/aircraft-register/api` is NOT API documentation. It is the aircraft register record for the aircraft carrying registration mark VH-API. Do not treat it as a developer surface.
- The RPAS Platform feeds named in the Operating Rules require authentication with a CASA-issued service account; they are not anonymously reachable.
- services.casa.gov.au and data.casa.rpasplatform.net return HTTP 403 to unadorned programmatic clients; a browser-like client is required.
